# Lending Disclosure Policy
version: 1.6

## 1. Scope
This policy covers every digital loan offered through the PayNova app and its lending partners.

## 2. Key Fact Statement
controls: CTRL_KFS_GEN
Every borrower receives a Key Fact Statement before signing the loan agreement. It lists the loan amount, tenure, repayment schedule, the all-in APR and every fee.

## 3. Annual Percentage Rate
controls: CTRL_APR_DISC, CTRL_KFS_GEN
The APR shown in the app and in the Key Fact Statement includes processing fees and insurance premiums. Penal charges are shown separately. No fee that is missing from the Key Fact Statement may be charged.

## 4. Cooling-off period
controls: CTRL_APR_DISC
A borrower may exit a loan within 3 days of disbursal by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR, with no penalty.
