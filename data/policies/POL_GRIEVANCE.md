# Grievance Redressal Policy
version: 2.3

## 1. Scope
This policy covers complaints from UPI users and borrowers, received in the app, by email or by phone.

## 2. Nodal grievance officer
controls: CTRL_GRIEVANCE_WF
PayNova names a nodal grievance officer. Their name, email and phone number are shown in the app and on the website.

## 3. Resolution timeline
controls: CTRL_GRIEVANCE_WF
Every complaint is acknowledged within 2 days and resolved within 30 days. If it is not resolved in time, the customer is told they can escalate to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.

## 4. Reporting
Customer Support sends a monthly complaints summary to the Board.
