# KYC Policy
version: 4.2

## 1. Scope
This policy covers customer identification for UPI accounts and digital loans.

## 2. Verification at onboarding
controls: CTRL_KYC_VERIFY
Customers are verified through Aadhaar OTP e-KYC or video-based customer identification (V-CIP). PAN is mandatory before any loan is disbursed.

## 3. Periodic updation
controls: CTRL_KYC_VERIFY
KYC is refreshed every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

## 4. Record keeping
KYC records are retained as set out in the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
