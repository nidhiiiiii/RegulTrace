# Incident Response Policy
version: 3.0

## 1. Scope
This policy covers security incidents and personal data breaches affecting PayNova systems.

## 2. Detection and logging
controls: CTRL_AUDITLOG
Security logs from all production systems are retained for 180 days in the data warehouse and reviewed daily.

## 3. Reporting cyber incidents
controls: CTRL_BREACH_NOTIF
Cyber incidents are reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of detection. Incidents affecting payment systems are also reported to RBI within 6 hours.

## 4. Personal data breach
controls: CTRL_BREACH_NOTIF
If personal data is breached, PayNova informs the Data Protection Board and affected customers without delay, and sends a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours.
