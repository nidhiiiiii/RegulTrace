# Consent Management Policy
version: 2.0

## 1. Scope
This policy covers how PayNova asks for, records and honours customer consent to process personal data.

## 2. Notice and consent at onboarding
controls: CTRL_CONSENT_CAP
Before collecting personal data, the app shows an itemised notice in English and Hindi listing each purpose. Consent is recorded per purpose with a timestamp in the consent service.

## 3. Withdrawal of consent
controls: CTRL_CONSENT_CAP
Customers can withdraw consent in the app at any time. Processing for the withdrawn purpose stops within 7 days, and erasure follows the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

## 4. Minors
PayNova does not open accounts for anyone under 18.
