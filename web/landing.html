Why a graph

Search finds documents. RegulTrace finds people.

A search for "erasure" returns the retention policy. It can't tell you that the engineer who owns the deletion job has to act, because that name never appears next to the word. That connection only exists in how your company is wired together.

Every answer comes with the path through your company and the query that found it.
