How it works

Four steps, no spreadsheets

No more "does this affect you?" email chains and applicability matrices that go stale the week they're updated.

A rule changes

RBI publishes a circular, or you paste one in.

The graph finds it

FalkorDB retrieves the affected sections and everything wired to them.

The fix is written

Exact before-and-after wording for each policy section.

Owners act

Each responsible person gets their task, before the deadline.

Why a graph

Search tells you which documents mention the new rule. It can't tell you that the engineer who owns the deletion job has to act, because that name never appears next to the word "erasure". That connection only exists in how your company is wired together. That's a graph.
